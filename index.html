About Us

We are undergraduate and research students at the International Institute of Information Technology, Hyderabad. Electronics, software, structures and communications come together in one team, and we build complete space-mission systems from scratch, from the first sketch to launch day.

What We Build

Our work is organised into five subsystems. Members design, manufacture and test each one in-house, then integrate them into a single vehicle.

Structure

Mechanical design of the vehicle body, internal layout and separation mechanisms, developed with CAD and 3D printing.

CAD · 3D printing · Mechanisms · Materials

Electronics

Flight computer, sensors, actuators and power systems, along with circuit design, wiring and board assembly.

Microcontrollers · Sensors · Actuators · PCB design

Flight Software

Embedded code that reads sensor data, determines the flight phase, controls actuators and logs data for later analysis.

Embedded programming · State machines · Control systems

Communications & Ground Station

Radio link design, antenna construction and a ground station application for viewing telemetry and tracking position.

Radio links · Antennas · Telemetry · Data visualisation

Descent & Guidance

Design, sewing and testing of parachutes and gliding surfaces, together with algorithms for steering and navigation.

Aerodynamics · Fabrication · Navigation
